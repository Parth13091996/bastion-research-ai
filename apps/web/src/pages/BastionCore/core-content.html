Questions, answered.

How do I subscribe?

Choose a plan, create an account or sign in, and complete the required onboarding, verification, agreement and payment steps on Bastion Research. Already a member? Manage your plan in your subscription settings.

How do the three-month and annual plans differ?

Both plans include CORE research, past research, Scratchpad, premium webinars and priority updates while your subscription is active. The introductory plan costs ₹5,000 including GST for 3 months. The annual plan costs ₹18,750 including GST for 12 months.

Does “One Time” mean lifetime access?

No. It is a paid, three-month introductory subscription that can be purchased only once. After it expires, renew with the annual plan. It does not provide lifetime access.

Can I renew the annual plan more than once?

Yes. The once-only purchase rule applies to the three-month introductory plan. The annual plan can be renewed repeatedly for continued access.

What happens when my subscription expires?

Premium research and features become locked when your subscription expires. Renew your annual subscription to regain access. Research coverage and follow-up updates are available to you during an active subscription.

Can I see sample research or get a free trial?

There is no free trial of the paid CORE plan. You can create a free account or sign in to view the research made available to free members before deciding whether to subscribe.

What will I receive as a subscriber?

Detailed business notes, research ideas, quarterly tracking, relevant company and corporate-action updates, past research, Scratchpad access and premium webinars. We explain the investment thesis, key risks and changes in our view through the research journey, with access tied to your subscription validity.

How often are new ideas published?

There is no fixed quota or promised schedule for new ideas. Research is shared when we find an opportunity that meets our process. We also track and update companies already under coverage.

What does CORE not provide?

CORE does not provide a ready-made model portfolio, personalized portfolio management, intraday or F&O trading calls, or guaranteed returns. Research discusses estimated fair value through scenarios rather than promising a target price or upside.

Is the research limited to particular sectors or market caps?

Bastion CORE is sector and market-cap agnostic. The focus is on understanding the business, management, accounting quality, valuations and business tailwinds through the SMART framework.

Are subscriptions refundable?

Subscription payments are non-refundable under the current Refund Policy. Please review the service details and available sample research before subscribing. For access or billing help, email connect@bastionresearch.in.

How can I contact the team?

Email connect@bastionresearch.in or use the Contact Us page for service and subscription questions.
